### Description

Theoretical aspects of Operating systems: memory management, process management, I/O management, and information management. Issues on Job Control Language, Assembler, Loader, and Link. Example cases of operating systems, compilers, interpreters and utilities.

Understand the concept of operating systems, able to understand small, basic Linux kernel code.
