# Assessment #1

## Problem Statement

A company wants to expand its internal data storage. Therefore, the
owner of the company has entrusted you to decide between:

1.  **Building your own data storage server**, or
2.  **Renting the storage from another company**.

The details of the cost of both options are as follows.

---

## Option 1: Building Your Own Data Storage Server

### Initial Costs

- **Purchase of Data Storage Server:** 7,800,000 Baht
- **Room for placing the Data Storage Server:** 700,000 Baht

### Server Lifetime

- **Lifetime of the storage server:** 10 years

### Operating Costs

- **Operating costs** (maintenance, electricity, and staff): 550,000
  Baht per year
- **Annual increase in operating costs:** 35,000 Baht per year

### System Update Costs

- The first system update occurs in **Year 4**.
- The system update is repeated **every 4 years**.
- **Cost per system update:** 150,000 Baht

### Salvage Value

- **After the end of its useful life:** The server can be sold for
  **10% of the purchase price**.
- **If sold before the end of its useful life:** The server can be
  sold for **25% of the purchase price**.

---

## Project Information

- **Project investment period:** 15 years
- **Minimum Attractive Rate of Return (MARR):** 18% per year
- **Analysis starting point:** Present time, **t = 0**

---

## Required

**Show the present worth at the present time, t = 0.**
