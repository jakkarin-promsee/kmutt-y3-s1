## Course scope (from syllabus)

Theoretical basis of ML through examples + hands-on coding. Techniques across **supervised,
unsupervised, and reinforcement** learning: statistical learning, model training, kernel machines,
regression & generalized linear models, tree & ensemble models, dimensionality reduction,
clustering, neural networks, deep learning, and applications.

CLOs: **CLO1** supervised-learning algorithms · **CLO2** unsupervised + reinforcement learning ·
**CLO3** implement ML models & workflows (vision, representation learning, anomaly detection).

Because of policy #3–#4 and the root "teach me" rule: for graded homework, **help me learn, don't
just hand me an answer to submit.** Explain the concept, walk through the method, check my
reasoning, and let me write the final solution. Point me to the relevant ISLP/Géron section. Full
worked code/solutions are fine for *practice* problems and for understanding — just flag when
something would be my gradable deliverable so I keep it honest.
